Common questions

How do I add a course?

Home → Add Course → fill in the title, provider, date, credits and category → Save. Your progress ring updates instantly.

What is free, and what is Pro?

Free: 1 license, up to 10 courses, full progress tracking. Pro ($9.99/month or $59.99/year): unlimited licenses and courses, certificate vault, smart reminders and PDF reports. Renewal Pass ($19.99 one-time): full Pro for 90 days, no subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Profile → Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID / account you bought with.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. This deletes your licenses, courses, reminder settings, subscription records and certificate files. Security audit logs may be retained for up to 12 months (see the Privacy Policy).

Is TrackCE legal advice?

No. TrackCE is a tracking tool. Always verify your requirements with your licensing board.

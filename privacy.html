Privacy Policy — TrackCE

Last updated: 24 November 2026

TrackCE ("we", "our", "the app") is operated by Sana Parween (SS & Co) (contact: sanavibes.in@gmail.com). This policy explains what the TrackCE app collects, why, and your choices. The app is available at ssandco.github.io/trackce-site.

What we collect

  1. Account information. When you sign in with Google, Apple or email, we receive your email address and a unique account ID from the sign-in provider (Firebase Authentication). We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  2. License and course records you enter. Profession, state, license number (optional), renewal dates, course titles, providers, categories, credit hours and completion dates. This is the core data the app needs to track your progress.
  3. Certificate files (Pro, optional). If you use the certificate vault, the certificate photos/PDFs you upload are stored in private cloud storage (Amazon S3 or compatible) under your account only.
  4. Subscription status. Whether you are on the Free or Pro plan, the product purchased and its expiry date. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat — we never see or store your card details.
  5. Device push token (optional). If you enable reminders, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send renewal reminders to your device.
  6. Basic technical logs. Server logs (IP address, timestamps, request paths) kept for security and reliability, and an audit log of events such as subscription changes and account deletion.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than certificates you choose to upload), health data, or advertising identifiers, and we do not run third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

How we use it

  • To provide CE tracking: computing your progress, category totals and renewal countdowns.
  • To send the reminders you turned on.
  • To unlock Pro features after a purchase is verified.
  • To keep the service secure (rate limiting, abuse prevention, audit logs).
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and push notifications.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and purchase verification.
  • Render — app servers, database and certificate file storage.
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and in-app purchase processing.

Each provider only receives the data needed to do its job.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while your account exists. If you delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), your licenses, courses, reminder settings, subscription records and certificate files are deleted from our systems. Security audit logs may be retained for up to 12 months.

Your rights

You can view and edit your records in the app at any time, and delete your account and all associated data from Profile → Delete account. You may also email sanavibes.in@gmail.com to request a copy of your data or its deletion. If you are in a region with additional privacy rights (such as the EU/UK GDPR or California CCPA/CPRA), those rights apply — including access, correction, deletion and portability — and we will honour requests within the time the law requires.

Children

TrackCE is for licensed professionals and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS/TLS, access to records is restricted to your account, certificate downloads use short-lived signed links, and servers verify your sign-in on every request. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of a breach where the law requires it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page, change the date at the top, and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
